The Commitments of Traders reports are released at 3:30 pm Washington D.C. time. The "futures only" and "futures and options combined" reports are usually released Friday. The release usually includes data from the previous Tuesday. Due to federal holidays, reports may be released one day later. Please recall that Good Friday is not a federal holiday. The following is a tentative schedule of releases through 2006. Please check this site regularly for possible changes.
