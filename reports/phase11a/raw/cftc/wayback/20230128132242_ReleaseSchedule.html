Release Schedule

The Commitments of Traders reports are released at 3:30 p.m. Eastern time. The Futures Only reports and Futures and Options Combined reports are usually released on Friday. The release usually includes data from the previous Tuesday. The following is a tentative schedule of releases through 2023. Federal holidays may delay release by one or two days.

2022

Dates
December 02 09 16 23 30

 

2023

Dates
