Commitments of Traders - Release Schedule Dates for 2005

Backgrounder - The Commitments of Traders Report

Contains general background information and detailed explanatory notes for the short form, the long form, and the options-and-futures-combined reports. Reports are published for contract markets in which 20 or more traders hold positions equal to or above the reporting levels established by the CFTC.

****Special Announcement****

December 10, 2004: Beginning with the Commitments of Traders (COT) release of January 7, 2005, web page links to a new NYBT (New York Board of Trade) report will replace the current links to the separate CSCE, NYCE and NYFE reports. The NYBT has completed the merger of its component exchanges into the one exchange. The new COT reports will reflect this organization of NYBT. The 2005 historical files will also reflect this exchange consolidation by incorporating the name "New York Board of Trade" in its data. Historical files through year 2004 will not be affected by this change.
