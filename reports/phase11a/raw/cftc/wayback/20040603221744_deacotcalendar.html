The Commitments of Traders reports are released at 3:30 pm Washington D.C. time. The "futures only" and the "futures and options combined" reports are usually released Friday. The release usually includes data from the previous Tuesday. Due to holidays reports may be released one day later. The following is the current release schedule. Please check this site regularly for possible changes.
