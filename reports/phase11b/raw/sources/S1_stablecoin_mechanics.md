# S1 — Stablecoin Mechanics Audit: USDT and USDC Chain-Level Reconstructability

**Phase:** 11B Stage 0 (audit only — not a backtest, no price/return/supply data acquired)
**Scope:** Documentation, verified contract source, issuer announcements, and news of discrete events only.

**Status legend:** `VERIFIED_CODE` (read directly from verified/canonical contract source), `VERIFIED_DOC` (read directly from the issuer's or protocol operator's own primary document/page), `SECONDARY` (independent journalism / third-party technical write-up, not the primary source itself, or a primary source reached only via a summarizing fetch rather than saved raw), `UNVERIFIED` (could not be confirmed within this audit's scope/budget — flagged, not used to support any conclusion).

**Explicit compliance note:** No stablecoin supply, circulation, price, OHLCV, or market-cap time series values were read, recorded, or used anywhere in this report. Where a source page (e.g. Tether's or Circle's transparency page) necessarily displays current point-in-time reserve/liability totals, only the **structure and methodology** of that page is described below; specific dollar figures observed while retrieving those pages are deliberately omitted, including from the raw snapshot analysis narrative (the raw HTML snapshot itself is retained on disk per the task's snapshot instruction, but its numeric content is not transcribed into this report or used in any classification judgment).

| Fact | Value | Status | Source | Retrieval date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| USDT chain-swap mechanism | An authorized counterparty (or Tether itself) mints on a destination chain and burns/redeems an equivalent amount on a source chain; Tether has stated total global supply is not intended to change as a result | SECONDARY | General secondary reporting (CryptoSlate, Forex Crunch, CryptoMode) | 2026-09-26 |

| What exactly must be netted, and does it require labels? | To compute "new dollars entering the stablecoin system" from raw per-chain supply data, one must subtract: (a) chain-swap mint/burn pairs (USDT), (b) CCTP burn/mint pairs (USDC), and (c) bridge lock/mint pairs (USDC.e, USDT0, other L2 bridges). ## FINAL

**Question:** Is a deterministic chain-level aggregate of USDT+USDC **net new issuance** (excluding chain swaps, bridge mints, and CCTP transfers) producible from raw chain data **without third-party labels**, for 2019–2024?

**Answer: No.** Gross per-contract `totalSupply` is exactly and deterministically reconstructable from raw Ethereum event logs alone for both tokens (Section 4), but that quantity is **not** "net new issuance" — it includes chain-swap mints (USDT), CCTP round-trips (USDC, from April 2023), and bridge lock/mint round-trips (both, at varying dates). Separating those components from organic new-dollar issuance requires knowledge external to the bare event stream:

- For **USDC**, the necessary label (which minter/contract addresses are CCTP `TokenMessenger` contracts, or bridge lockbox contracts) is itself **publicly documented and stable** — Circle publishes these addresses per chain in its own developer documentation, and equivalent lockbox addresses for major L2 bridges are public and rarely change. This does not require a paid vendor, but it is **not derivable from the chain data alone**; it requires reading an external, issuer/protocol-published reference list.
- For **USDT**, the equivalent labels (which specific `issue()`/`redeem()` calls correspond to a chain swap, or the full, current, and historical list of Tether treasury addresses) are **not** systematically published as a stable machine-readable reference for the full 2019–2024 span. What exists instead is a scattered history of ad hoc issuer tweets/press releases (each giving an approximate amount and rough date, as in the documented 2019 300M Omni→Ethereum swap) plus a **third-party** (Etherscan) address attribution for "the" treasury address that this audit could not confirm as issuer-disclosed, complete, or stable over time.

Because the combined USDT+USDC aggregate inherits USDT's weaker dependency, the overall "net new issuance excluding swaps/bridges/CCTP, 2019–2024" aggregate classifies as:

**D — PIT_LABEL_DEPENDENT**, with the USDC-only sub-component more precisely classifiable as **B — PUBLIC_VENDOR_REPRODUCIBLE** (reproducible by any researcher using Circle's own published, stable, free contract-address documentation, but not from chain data in isolation) and the USDT chain-swap-netting sub-component closer to **D**, bordering **E — PROPRIETARY** if a complete historical treasury-address list is only obtainable from a paid on-chain-analytics vendor (e.g. Arkham, Nansen, Chainalysis) rather than from Tether's own disclosures.

### Narrower quantities, classified separately

| Quantity | Classification | Why | Migration/contamination and dates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ethereum-only USDC `totalSupply`** | **A — RAW_CHAIN_RECONSTRUCTABLE** | Exact event-log replay of `Mint`/`Burn` (or equivalently `Transfer(0x0,...)`/`Transfer(...,0x0)`) from contract creation, no labels needed, no archive state needed (Section 4) | The **number** is exact, but its **interpretation as new issuance** is contaminated from **2023-04-26 onward** by CCTP V1 mint/burn round-trips (expanding further with CCTP V2 from **2025-03-11**), and from **2023-06 through 2023-10** by the native-USDC rollouts on Arbitrum/Optimism/Base/Polygon PoS, which caused some Ethereum-side burns/mints as liquidity relocated relative to the pre-existing bridged USDC.e representations. The **Tron discontinuation (minting halted 2024-02-21, migration deadline 2025-02)** likely shows up as elevated Ethereum-side mints from migrating Tron holders, again not organic new demand |
| **Ethereum-only USDT `totalSupply` minus Tether treasury balance** | **D — PIT_LABEL_DEPENDENT** (for the subtraction step); the `totalSupply` term alone is **A** | `totalSupply` itself replays exactly from `Issue`/`Redeem`/`DestroyedBlackFunds` events (Section 4). But "minus treasury balance" requires a treasury address, and the only one located in this audit is a **third-party (Etherscan) label**, not a confirmed, complete, issuer-published list — Tether is understood to operate multiple treasury-related addresses across chains and over time, so a single-address subtraction is a structural approximation even before considering whether the label itself is trustworthy or historically stable | Contaminated further by (a) the **2019 Omni→Ethereum chain-swap era** (large, irregular treasury-balance swings driven by cross-chain rebalancing rather than demand, as directly documented for the 300M 2019-10-29 swap), (b) ongoing Tron↔Ethereum swap activity throughout 2019–2024, and (c) the fact that `destroyBlackFunds` reduces `totalSupply` for law-enforcement/compliance seizures unrelated to issuance economics |

**Overall conclusion for Phase 11B Stage 0:** a mechanically deterministic, fully label-free reconstruction of *economically meaningful* USDT+USDC net new issuance is not available for 2019–2024. Gross per-chain `totalSupply` is label-free and exact but is a contaminated proxy whose contamination sources (chain swaps, CCTP, bridge migrations) are each independently datable (see the specific 2023-04-26, 2023-06/09/10, 2024-02-21, and 2025-03-11 milestones above) but not independently *netted out* without external, and in USDT's case non-systematic, labels. This is a data-availability finding, not a mechanism-strength finding; whether it warrants proceeding to a labeled/vendor-dependent construction (raising the classification to C/D deliberately, with the associated caveats) is a decision for the next stage of Phase 11B, not this audit.
