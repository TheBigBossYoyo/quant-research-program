A. NETWORK ACTIVITY
1. Liu, Y. & Tsyvinski, A. "Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency". NBER WP 24877 (Aug 2018 draft).
   https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877 ; published RFS 34(6):2689-2727 (2021), DOI 10.1093/rfs/hhaa113.
   Local: liu_tsyvinski_nber_w24877.pdf. Table 30 p.29: BTC wallet-user price-to-dividend ratio, daily t+1..t+7,
   coefficients 0.13/0.05/-0.13/-0.12/0.05/0.09/0.05, R2 0.00 throughout [re-checked]. Published abstract (EconPapers):
   returns "exposed to" network factors; momentum and attention "forecast" returns.
2. Liu, Y., Tsyvinski, A. & Wu, X. "Common Risk Factors in Cryptocurrency". NBER WP 25882 (Dec 2019 draft);
   JF 77(2):1133-1177 (2022), DOI 10.1111/jofi.13119. Local: liu_tsyvinski_wu_nber_w25882.pdf. The draft contains no
   address variable; a later price-to-new-address result is known only via Cong et al. fn 11 (SECONDARY).

   2014-01-22..2021-01-04, IntoTheBlock core sample; RHS intercept t 1.82 (p.18) [re-checked].

  will not contain historical PIT data prior to that date"; before July 2025 PIT tracking limited to BTC, ETH and
  select tokens for listed metrics.

  values are fixed; newly discovered addresses affect only intervals on or after their discovery date".
