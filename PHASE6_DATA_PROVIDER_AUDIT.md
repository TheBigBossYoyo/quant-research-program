## 2. Per-provider verdict

- Norgate Data, US Stocks Platinum: the only candidate that passes every requirement that matters for this program. Delisted securities since 1950 with continuous series across symbol changes; vendor-maintained historical index constituents (Russell 3000 from July 1990, S&P 500 from 1957) that provide a point-in-time universe reference; unadjusted and total-return price modes; turnover, shares, market cap and GICS fields; ETFs since 1993; an EULA that explicitly permits personal backtesting and retention of derived results. Weaknesses: no delisting reason or delisting return (a conservative preregistered treatment is required, see PHASE6_UNIVERSE_CONSTRUCTION.md); Windows-only updater that must be running while Python reads the database (acceptable here); no monthly billing (6 or 12 months up front); the delisted count is stated as of Sep 2022 and completeness through 2026 is UNVERIFIED until reconciled after purchase. Price fits the ceiling on the 12-month term (EUR 45.06/month equivalent).
- EODHD All-World (USD 19.99): the cheapest plausible option; it carries delisted tickers with full history, unadjusted close, splits and dividends. It fails the point-in-time membership requirement (S&P 500 constituents only from April 2012 and only with the USD 59.99 plan), has no historical market cap, no documented symbol-change continuity, no delisting dates on the price plan, and an undocumented pre-2018 delisting depth. A point-in-time universe would have to be rebuilt from lagged liquidity alone with no vendor membership series to reconcile against. Adequate fallback if Norgate is refused; not the first choice.
- Tiingo Power (USD 30): measured inactive-ticker coverage begins materially only around 2013-2015, so any test including the dot-com aftermath or 2008 would be survivorship-biased. FAIL.
- Massive (ex-Polygon): 20+ years only on the USD 199 tier; Developer (10 years) is over budget and too short. FAIL.
- Sharadar SEP: methodologically credible by reputation, but pricing is shown only after account creation, which this agent may not perform. UNVERIFIED; not recommendable without a price.
- FMP: 30-year history only from the USD 69 Professional tier (over budget); delisted-company log exists, price depth for delisted names UNVERIFIED. FAIL on budget.
- Free ticker downloads (Yahoo, Stooq): survivors only, silently restated adjustments, no delistings, licence problems. FAIL as evidence; permitted for parser/engineering tests only (mandate section 5).
- Kenneth French data library (free): CRSP-derived, survivorship-free portfolio-level returns (momentum deciles, short-term reversal deciles, variance and residual-variance portfolios, industry portfolios, factors, breakpoints), daily and monthly, back to 1926/1963, with the CRSP vintage stated in each file. It cannot support stock-level portfolio construction, position counts, Trading 212 costs or capacity, and its equal-weighted series are microcap-dominated. It CAN support family-level Stage 1-2 diagnostics (does the effect exist, is it monotonic, is it era-stable) at institutional data quality and zero cost. Used in Phase 6 strictly for FACTOR_EVIDENCE-level classification, never for PORTFOLIO_COMPONENT_CANDIDATE or above.

## 3. Answer to the gate question

Hedge-fund-grade, survivorship-safe, point-in-time stock-level research cannot proceed without a purchase. Free ticker-level sources fail the survivorship and adjustment requirements; the only free survivorship-safe source (French library) is portfolio-level. A purchase is therefore necessary for Stages 3-7 of the funnel. The exact recommendation is in PHASE6_DATA_PURCHASE_RECOMMENDATION.md. Stages 1-2 (family-level) proceed on the French library in the meantime under the same chronological lock (PHASE6_EQUITY_DATA_LOCK.md).
