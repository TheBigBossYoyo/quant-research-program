# PHASE4_RED_TEAM_REPORT - frozen E040 on twelve preregistered perpetuals, 2022-2024

Written 2026-09-09 after the supplemented replication (E044_20260909T135755) and its analysis (E045_20260909T140237) were frozen. Verdict under the preregistered gate: CROSS_ASSET_WEAK_SIGNAL, DO_NOT_OPEN_2025 (see PHASE4_PREVALIDATION_DECISION.md). Both cases below are recorded; neither changes the strategy or the gate.

## RESEARCHER CASE

1. The mechanism was frozen first and then transported unchanged to twelve names chosen with information available at the end of 2021, including two later losers among the majors (LTC, LINK) and one terminated contract (MATIC). Eight of twelve are net positive after 9.05 bps per side plus actual funding; nine of twelve have positive risk-adjusted returns; nine of twelve have positive factor intercepts.
2. The cross-asset portfolio is not a beta trade. Equal-weight net exposure averages -3.9% (gross 43.6%), underlying betas are 0.08-0.36, BTC betas are slightly negative after controlling the underlying, and in 2022, when the twelve underlyings lost 53-94%, the primary portfolio returned +6.2% with seven of twelve sleeves positive and the trend sleeve net short on average.
3. The primary portfolio (Sharpe 0.87, CAGR 21.5%, drawdown -24.1%) is not carried by one asset, one year or a handful of trades: the top trade is 3.3% of positive P&L, the top five 13.2%, removing the best year leaves +34.7%, removing the best five trade contributions leaves Sharpe 0.54. Eleven of twelve gates pass.
4. Costs do not kill it: at 14 bps per side it keeps Sharpe 0.74; one extra 4h delay keeps 0.76; adverse funding of +1 bp per settlement keeps 0.69; the positive-asset count is 8 of 12 in every case.
5. Diversification is real relative to the underlyings: average pairwise strategy correlation 0.39 (trend 0.40, breakout 0.22) against 0.64 for the assets themselves; the causal inverse-volatility component version reaches Sharpe 0.91 with a -15.9% drawdown.
6. The result is comparable to, not better than, the discovery assets on the same corrected engine and window (BTC/ETH 50/50 E040: Sharpe 0.96, CAGR 24.2%, drawdown -22.2%), which is what an honest replication of a modest effect should look like.

## RED-TEAM CASE

1. **Crypto beta timing, not alpha.** Factor loadings are small, but the money was made in two synchronized rallies: seven of the ten largest portfolio trade contributions are trend positions opened in October-November 2023 or October-November 2024, all long. Rolling BTC beta is low because the rule is flat or short in between; the P&L timing is nonetheless a bet on the same two events across all twelve names. Yearly median factor alpha is -14% (2022), +184% (2023), -83% (2024): the "alpha" is one year of a three-year sample.
2. **Concentration in 2024 and in Q4s.** The top calendar year contributes 56.4% of positive portfolio P&L against a 60% limit; per asset, the top year is 48-100% of positive P&L (100% for XRP, BNB, LINK, whose only good year is 2024) and the top five trades are 36-66%. Individual sleeves are as concentrated as the Phase 3 red team warned; only the portfolio diversifies it.
3. **Dependence.** Twelve correlated names in one regime are not twelve experiments. Resampling dates jointly, the median alpha's interval is [-26%, +137%] per year and the median Sharpe's is [-0.15, +1.40]; the positive-fraction interval is [0.42, 1.00]. The asset-only bootstrap ([+5%, +124%]) looks significant precisely because it wrongly treats the names as independent. The one failing gate is the one that respects dependence.
4. **The factor "alpha" is a conditional extrapolation.** The preregistered regression includes lagged BTC 30-day volatility and a lagged trend-state sign as regressors; the intercept is therefore the expected return at zero volatility and neutral trend state, which never occurs. That is why per-asset intercepts of 100-250% per year appear next to realized CAGRs of 30-50%. The underlying-only intercept (median 28.5% per year, nine of twelve positive, every interval including zero) is the interpretable number. The gate was applied as preregistered, but no reader should quote the conditional intercept as an excess return.
5. **One component collapsed.** The compression breakout that gave BTC/ETH Sharpe 1.10/0.82 gives a cross-asset median Sharpe of 0.18, positive net in only seven of twelve names, median factor intercept 13% per year, univariate intercept 5%, and contributes 24% of portfolio P&L. What generalizes is the slow-trend sleeve (median Sharpe 0.65, nine of twelve positive Sharpe) with 46-91% sleeve drawdowns. The E040 ensemble story ("two independent mechanisms") is weaker on the replication set than on the discovery pair.
6. **The pass set is fragile.** Positive fraction passes at exactly 2/3; supplementing five days of data moved XRP from a +0.13 to a -0.02 Sharpe. Had XRP been counted with LTC and LINK before supplementation it would have been the same 8 of 12 for a different reason. Top-year share passes by 3.6 points. Small perturbations of any of these flip the classification between WEAK_SIGNAL and MIXED_REPLICATION.
7. **Survivorship and selection.** The 2021 liquidity cohort avoids picking today's winners, but the archived-contract inventory is not guaranteed exhaustive, and this panel was already used for other Phase 1-2 signal research (different signals, same data), so it is not a pristine unseen cross-section. Two of the twelve are not tradeable today in their original form (MATIC terminated 2024-09; FTM marked SETTLING in the 2026-09-08 filter snapshot): the universe that replicated is partly a universe that no longer exists.
8. **Frozen-universe selection bias in the other direction.** Liquidity-ranked large caps of 2021 include exactly the names that had the strongest 2023-2024 recoveries (SOL, AVAX, DOGE, ADA); a cohort selected one year later or one year earlier would contain different names. There is no second cohort to test this.
9. **Funding and marks.** Funding settlement marks are hourly mark-price opens, with 6-9 settlements per asset falling back to trade opens; SOL has 49 off-schedule settlements. The adverse funding stress (+1 bp per settlement) costs 0.18 Sharpe on the portfolio, so funding assumptions matter at the margin. Exact settlement marks were never available.
10. **Supplementation artefacts.** The supplemented rerun changed only the four affected names and reproduced the other ten to the last digit, and the supplements were retrieved before any first-pass result existed. The first pass, however, was materially wrong for four names (forced flat for 238 bars each), which shows the discovery engine silently distorts results on incomplete inputs; nothing guarantees the BTC/ETH discovery histories were free of subtler input defects.
11. **MATIC treatment.** The 08:00 UTC pre-settlement exit is contract administration, but it also truncates a losing 2024 for MATIC (its best year was 2023) and the cash sleeve dilutes the denominator thereafter; both effects are small but favourable.
12. **Execution assumptions.** Fills at the next 4h open on hourly-candle opens, without spread or depth verification for alt perpetuals in 2022 (when several of these books were thin). The stress cases cover cost levels, not intra-bar gaps, exchange outages or forced deleveraging, and exposure drifts to 1.2-1.7x between resize events on the trend sleeve without any liquidation model.
13. **Correlation dependence.** Strategy correlations rise from 0.39 on average to 0.44 on stress days (BTC down more than 5% or all of 2022), drawdown correlations reach 0.89 for the DOT/ADA/AVAX/AXS cluster, and a multi-asset correlation cluster exists on 68% of days. The portfolio's -24% drawdown is a diversified outcome of a single regime, and the block-bootstrap conditional risk gives a 35% frequency of >30% drawdowns and 8% of >40% for the primary portfolio.
14. **Capital.** At EUR 500 the trend sleeve cannot be traded on LTC/LINK (99-100% of orders below the 20 USDT minimum notional) or on AVAX/BNB/AXS (whole-coin lots); the minimum practical capital is roughly 12.6-31.5k USDT depending on rounding tolerance. A strategy that cannot be run at the research budget cannot be paper-traded at that budget either.

## Evidence-weighted conclusion

The frozen rule did not fail: a majority of an out-of-sample cross-section is positive, the portfolio diversifies, survives cost, delay, funding and concentration removals, and behaves like a low-beta trend strategy in the 2022 bear. But it did not pass either. The only gate that accounts for the fact that twelve coins in 2022-2024 are one sample says the median effect is indistinguishable from zero, the breakout leg did not transport, the fragile margins on two other gates show how thin the evidence is, and the return depends on two synchronized Q4 rallies. The classification CROSS_ASSET_WEAK_SIGNAL is the honest one. 2025 stays locked; the frozen rule stays frozen; if a further independent regime or a second cohort ever becomes available under the protocol, this replication is the benchmark it must beat, and the multiplicity of that later test must count this one.
