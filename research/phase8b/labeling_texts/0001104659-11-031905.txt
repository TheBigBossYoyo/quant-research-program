On May 23, 2011, TravelCenters of America LLC, or we, us or our, entered into an underwriting agreement with Citigroup Global Markets Inc., Morgan Keegan & Company, Inc. and UBS Securities LLC, as the representatives of the several underwriters named therein, relating to the issuance and sale by us of 10,000,000 of our common shares at a public offering price of $5.69 per share in an underwritten public offering. Pursuant to the underwriting agreement and as part of the offering, we granted the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to an additional 1,500,000 of our common shares. The above description of the underwriting agreement is only a summary, is not complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the underwriting agreement, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 1.1 hereto and incorporated by reference herein. Item 8.01 Other Events On May 27, 2011, we completed the sale of 10,000,000 of our common shares to the underwriters pursuant to the underwriting agreement referred to in Item 1.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K. After deducting underwriting discounts and commissions, we received proceeds of approximately $53.8 million, before expenses, from the sale of those shares.
