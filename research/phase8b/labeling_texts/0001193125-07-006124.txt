Brighton Ski Resort On January 9, 2007, we acquired the Brighton Ski Resort in Brighton, Utah from a subsidiary of Boyne USA, Inc. (Boyne), a national ski resort operator, for a purchase price of $35.0 million. The Brighton Ski Resort property consists of a permit interest in land owned by the United States National Forest Service, two leasehold-interest land parcels and two fee simple land parcels. The resort features two restaurants, a 20-room lodge and ski rental facility and a small retail village. The ski area features 850 skiable acres, 66 downhill slopes and 7 chairlifts. Affiliates of Boyne are currently lessees and operators of our Gatlinburg Sky Lift and Cypress Mountain properties. We have leased the ski resort back to Boyne to operate the property through an affiliate under two long-term, triple-net leases with initial terms of 20 years and four five-year renewal options. The leases are cross-defaulted with our Gatlinburg Sky Lift and Cypress Mountain leases. The minimum annual rent for the property is approximately $3.2 million in the initial year, increasing annually to approximately $3.9 million. Additional rent is a percentage of incremental total revenue over a specified threshold. Boyne also received the option to repurchase the property from us at a price that will result in a fixed return to us. The option is exercisable by Boyne beginning in the seventh year through the 25 th year following the acquisition.
