LLC and Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, as representatives of the underwriters listed therein (the Underwriters), pursuant to which the Company agreed to issue and sell 10,850,000 shares of the Companys common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the Common Stock), plus up to an additional 1,627,500 shares of Common Stock if the Underwriters exercise their option to purchase additional shares of Common Stock in full. The public offering price for the shares was $62.00 per share. The transaction closed on March 3, 2017. Total net proceeds of the offering (after deducting the underwriting discount and other estimated expenses) were approximately $645.5 million. The Company expects to use the net proceeds from the offering to repay a portion of the borrowings outstanding under its $2.0 billion revolving credit facility and, to the extent not used for that purpose, to fund potential investment opportunities and/or for other general corporate purposes.
