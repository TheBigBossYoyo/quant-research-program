# Experiment registry

## Registered E014 — survivorship-aware perpetual universe and cross-sectional mechanisms (preregistered 2026-09-08 before acquisition completes)

Why: on two mega-cap assets every short-horizon effect was cost-dominated (E001, E010, E012) and every daily effect under-powered (E003, E011, E013). Cross-sectional construction multiplies independent observations, is dollar-neutral by design, and tests distinct economics: relative-strength continuation or reversal across coins, dispersion in funding as a positioning/carry premium, and relative aggressive flow. Counterparties: leveraged directional traders chasing recent winners and paying funding; forced deleveraging in crowded names.

Data (ENV002): public GET-only monthly archives from data.binance.vision, USDT-quoted UM perpetuals, daily klines and funding rates, 2022-01 through 2024-12 only, SHA256-verified against the archive checksums and logged with provenance. The archive lists delisted contracts (verified: LUNAUSDT ends 2022-05), so membership is built from file existence, not from today's listing. Listing snapshot: data/metadata/um_archive_listing_20260908T181713.json (393 symbols with at least one development month, 23 delisted inside the window). 2025 is not downloaded at this stage; 2026 is never downloaded. Funding archives carry funding_interval_hours, which is respected (some contracts settle every 4 hours).

Point-in-time eligibility at signal day t (all from bars <= t): at least 60 daily bars observed; a bar exists on day t; trailing 30-day median quote volume >= 20 million USDT; close > 0. Delisting: a symbol whose next bar is absent is exited at its last available daily open with the stressed cost; no ghost holding. Prices near zero (LUNA-type collapses) are kept; returns are capped at -100% per position by construction of weight accounting.

Hypotheses (8, cumulative 199): H-A1/H-A2 cross-sectional momentum at 7-day and 30-day trailing return (long top quintile, short bottom quintile) and their reversal mirrors (4 hypotheses); H-B funding-dispersion: rank by trailing 7-day cumulative funding, short the top quintile and long the bottom quintile to collect funding on both legs, plus its mirror (2); H-C relative aggressive flow: rank by trailing 7-day taker imbalance from daily klines, continuation and reversal (2). Signal at the daily close of day t, weekly rebalance every seventh day, execution at the open of day t+2 (one full day latency, the standing convention), equal-weight dollar-neutral legs at 50% gross each (1.0x gross total, no leverage), positions held until the next rebalance or delisting.

Stage 1 (cheap, first): per rebalance date the cross-sectional Spearman IC between each ranking feature (ret7, ret30, fund7, flow7) and the following week's open-to-open return over eligible symbols; report the mean IC, the t-statistic from the time series of non-overlapping weekly ICs, IC by year and decay at 1/2/4 weeks. Gate: |t| exceeds the two-sided normal critical value at 0.05/4. A feature failing Stage 1 is not advanced; its two directional hypotheses still count.

Stage 2 (Stage-1 survivors only): weight-based portfolio accounting with funding cashflows (rate × close-proxy notional at each settlement, sign-correct for longs and shorts), costs per executed notional 7 bps base, 14 bps stress, 28 bps severe (alts are wider than BTC/ETH; friction is uncalibrated), turnover recorded. Baselines: equal-weight long of the eligible universe and BTC long, same rebalance. Gates: stressed (14 bps) net CAGR > 0, stressed daily Sharpe > 0, at least two of three positive stressed calendar years, stressed maximum daily drawdown above -30%. Severe 28 bps is descriptive.

Stage 3 (Stage-2 survivors): E011-style block bootstrap (7/30/60-day, 10000 samples, Bonferroni with the cumulative count), excess over the lagged short-rate reference, one extra day of delay, leave-one-year-out sign check, and a capital-feasibility note (minimum notionals, quantity steps at 500 USDT; a candidate that needs more capital says so rather than being distorted). No validation or final access. No retuning of quintile width, rebalance frequency, lookbacks or liquidity threshold after seeing results.

Falsification tests before results: point-in-time eligibility uses only bars <= t; a symbol absent after t is exited and never re-created; feature prefix invariance; weekly forward returns aligned to open(t+2)→open(t+9); funding sign and interval handling; dollar-neutral weights sum to zero with 1.0x gross; checksum-verified loader that refuses 2025+ files.

Completed ENV002 and E014_20260908T190826. Acquisition: 15584 items (7793 daily-kline and 7791 funding archives, 393 symbols, 233049 symbol-days, 823925 funding events of which 248561 at 4-hour and 276 at 2-hour intervals), all SHA256-verified, zero errors after a transient connection outage forced one resume from cache (logs universe_acquisition_20260908T182421/184107.jsonl). Data defect found and quarantined: seven daily rows on 2023-09-19/20 and 2023-11-30 (ARB, COMBO, DYDX, EGLD, GTC, MAGIC, THETA) report taker volume above total volume by 6–70%; the loader sets the taker field to NaN for those rows only, keeps prices/volume, reports them in integrity, and a regression test covers it. Stage 1 (critical |t| 2.50 at 0.05/4): ret7 IC -0.020 (t -1.37; 2022 +0.006, 2023 -0.045, 2024 -0.016); ret30 IC -0.031 (t -2.22; negative every year, flat decay to four weeks); fund7 IC -0.016 (t -1.36; 2022 +0.019, 2024 -0.042); flow7 IC +0.025 (t +1.94; positive every year, rising with horizon to +0.051 at four weeks). ## Registered E018 — cross-sectional risk and attention characteristics on the perpetual universe (preregistered 2026-09-08 before any result)

Mechanisms, distinct from return, funding and flow ranks already tested: (A) low-risk anomaly — leverage-constrained or lottery-seeking traders overpay for high-volatility coins, so low-volatility names earn higher risk-adjusted returns (feature vol30 = standard deviation of daily log returns over 30 days; both directions registered); (B) abnormal attention — unusually high recent volume marks attention-driven demand that either continues (spillover) or reverses (overreaction) (feature abnvol7 = mean quote volume over 7 days divided by mean over 90 days; both directions); (C) illiquidity premium — thinly traded names compensate holders (feature amihud30 = 30-day mean of |daily return| / quote volume; both directions). ## Registered E019 — market-wide positioning and breadth as a weekly BTC timing signal (preregistered 2026-09-08 before any result)

Mechanism: the perpetual universe is an information set about market-wide leveraged positioning and participation. When the average funding across all eligible contracts is high, the market is crowded long and vulnerable to deleveraging; when breadth (share of eligible names above their own 30-day close) is extreme, participation is either exhausted or capitulated. Distinct from E013 (single-asset funding on BTC/ETH) because the state variables are cross-sectional aggregates, and from E014–E018 because the traded instrument is BTC alone with a weekly cadence. Features at day t from eligible names only: agg_fund7 = cross-sectional mean of the 7-day cumulative funding rate; breadth30 = fraction of eligible names whose close is above their 30-day-ago close. Target: BTCUSDT perpetual open(t+2)→open(t+9). Four hypotheses (two features × two directions; cumulative 219). Stage 1: time-series Spearman IC of each feature against the BTC weekly forward return over the weekly rebalance calendar (about 253 observations), circular block bootstrap (4-week blocks, 2000 resamples, seed 20260908+400+k), gate 0.05/2 two-sided. Stage 2 for survivors: BTC signed exposure ±1.0x (long when the feature predicts up, short otherwise) with weekly re-evaluation through the same weight ledger, plus a long-flat variant (long or cash) counted as one additional trial per survivor; costs 7/14/28 bps; funding charged; gates as E016 plus Sharpe above BTC long. Survivors → E015 audit and E017 hostile items (start-2021, placebo by permuting the feature across weeks, breakeven cost). Tests before results: aggregates use only eligible names at t and are prefix-invariant; breadth is in [0,1]; target alignment.

Completed E019_20260908T194655 (56 tests pass; aggregate tests cover eligible-only averaging, the ten-name minimum, bounded breadth, prefix invariance and target alignment). # PHASE 3 — broad systematic alpha discovery (started 2026-09-09; prior conclusions preserved; economic-hypothesis count continues from 367)

## Phase 3 multiplicity framework (fixed before any Phase 3 result)

Three ledgers are kept from now on. (1) Economic hypotheses: a distinct mechanism/market/direction claim; continues the cumulative count (368 onward) and is the unit for family-wise control across families. (2) Architecture variants: coherent rule structures inside a family (for example dual-MA crossover vs MA-slope vs price/MA regime); within a family, architecture-level tests are controlled with Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.10 on the block-bootstrap p-values of net stressed Sharpe, and the Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey & López de Prado) is reported with N = number of architecture × asset × timeframe trials in the family and the empirical skew/kurtosis of the candidate's returns; a candidate advances only if DSR probability ≥ 0.95 under stressed costs. ## Registered E024 — liquidity-, positioning- and sweep-conditioned short-horizon signals on BTC/ETH perpetuals (preregistered 2026-09-08 before any return is observed)

Classification: retail-accessible short-horizon intraday systematic trading (decisions every minute, holding 5–60 minutes, taker execution with a 5-second latency allowance). Not HFT: no queue position, no passive fills are assumed in this branch.

Mechanisms and counterparties. F1 liquidity-normalised aggressive flow: net taker volume over the last 60 s divided by displayed depth within ±1% (latest bookDepth snapshot at or before t, age ≤ 120 s, otherwise NaN). Flow that is large relative to resting liquidity reflects urgency that displayed liquidity cannot absorb and should continue to move price (impact persistence); flow small relative to depth is absorbed and reverts. Counterparty: liquidity providers who under-price impact, and latecomers. F2 displayed-depth imbalance: (bid depth 1% − ask depth 1%)/(bid + ask) from the latest snapshot; a one-sided liquidity cushion predicts the direction of least resistance. F3 open-interest–price interaction (metrics window 2023-06-01..2024-03-03 only): ΔOI over 15 minutes relative to OI, multiplied by the sign of the 15-minute price change; positive values mean new positions entering in the direction of the move (crowding, predicted reversal), negative values mean covering (predicted continuation). F4 sweep pressure: signed notional of the largest aggregate trade in the last 60 s relative to the trailing 60-minute median of per-second maxima; a large sweep is temporary price pressure predicted to revert. ## Microstructure frontier — cost arithmetic recorded 2026-09-08

Using the forward-return dispersion of the sampled days, the extreme-decile edge implied by a rank correlation is roughly IC × σ_h × 1.75.
